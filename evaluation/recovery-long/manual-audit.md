# Extended-budget manual audit

These observations supplement the fixed automatic checks; they do not alter model prompts or as-run scores.

## Host interruptions

The first clean mode triple and the first handoff-crash multi-agent trial were affected by closed-lid sleep and repeated dark wakes. Completed model-call timing files show civil-time gaps much larger than their monotonic active elapsed time. Both backends rejected expired claims after waking; the initial single-agent integrator instead timed out with incomplete usage. These are environment-affected trials. Exclude whole matched condition/repetition triples, not selectively poor modes, when discussing ordinary performance. Preserve all original observations.

An external awake guard now pauses the outer matrix after a sleep gap and resumes only after 90 continuously awake seconds. This cannot rescue already expired in-flight claims, but prevents advancing through new trials during short dark wakes. An idle-sleep assertion remains attached to the matrix process; neither mechanism can stop closed-lid sleep.

## Recovery limitation exposed by real host sleep

Do not dismiss the sleep-affected rows only as timing noise. Both adapters fail fast when an unplanned pause expires the current claim; they do not automatically reclaim/recompute on `current completion rejected`. The controlled faults have explicit restart/reclaim logic, while arbitrary long host suspension does not. Comitia correctly fences stale completion, but coordination primitives alone do not make the surrounding workflow self-healing. Keep this as a substantive limitation while excluding the affected matched pairs from ordinary latency/quality comparisons.
