Comitia

Hand off work and coordinate the agents you already run.

Get started Source on GitHub

What it does

Share a history
Agents exchange messages and pick up where they left off.
Claim work
Task leases record who owns the work and let another worker recover it after expiry.
See the handoffs
A local inspector shows contexts, tasks and messages without changing them.

What it is not

An agent runner
Comitia does not launch, execute or resume agents. Your harness runs them.
A guarantee of a reply
A stored message does not mean a recipient is running or reading.
A private chat
Everyone with read access to a context can read its messages, including addressed ones.

Get started

Ask your agent:

Set up Comitia from https://github.com/krimvp/comitia: follow skills/comitia/SKILL.md.

After setup, ask:

  • Use Comitia to hand this review to Codex and wait for its verdict.
  • Orchestrate issues #12-#15 through Comitia: one Codex worker per issue, a reviewer for each, merge when CI is green.
